## 💡 Troubleshooting Tips

If you still experience trouble after running the fix, try these steps:

**Check hardware connections**
Ensure your headphones are plugged firmly into the correct port. If you use a USB headset, try a different USB port to rule out a broken connector.

**Check the playback device settings**
Click the speaker icon in your taskbar near the clock. Click the arrow button to view your playback devices. Make sure you selected your headphones and not your built-in monitor speakers.

**Update Windows**
Outdated system files interfere with hardware detection. Go to Settings, select Update & Security, and click Check for updates. Install all pending updates and restart your machine.

**Remove other audio software**
Some audio enhancement apps clash with Windows drivers. Disable or uninstall third-party equalizer applications before you run the Headphone-Fix-Tool again.
